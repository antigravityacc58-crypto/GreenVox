# Draft Konsep Paper GreenVox

## Working title
**GreenVox: Integrating Satellite-Derived Vegetation Monitoring and Student Citizen Science for School-Based Urban Greening**

## Core problem
Sekolah perkotaan membutuhkan cara sederhana untuk memahami kondisi ruang hijaunya dan mengubah informasi tersebut menjadi tindakan yang dapat dilakukan komunitas sekolah.

## Proposed novelty
GreenVox menggabungkan:
1. analisis vegetasi berbasis citra satelit,
2. WebGIS interaktif,
3. observasi lapangan oleh siswa (citizen science), dan
4. Green Action Loop untuk menghubungkan temuan ke aksi dan verifikasi.

## Research question
How can satellite-derived vegetation indicators combined with student citizen-science observations support school-based urban greening decisions?

## Data
- Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance: primary vegetation analysis.
- Landsat: optional historical/context analysis.
- Field observations: tree/canopy, grass, hardscape, exposed/open area.
- Study area: school AOI plus contextual buffer.

## Variables
- Mean/median NDVI by period.
- Percentage/area of selected vegetation class/threshold (define carefully).
- Count and category of field observations.
- Agreement/disagreement between satellite interpretation and ground observation.
- Optional awareness/participation indicators for social-movement evaluation.

## Important limitation
Satellite data does not by itself determine whether an area should be planted.
Recommendations must be validated with land use, safety, ownership, function, and field conditions.
